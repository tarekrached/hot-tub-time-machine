# Filter care

Research behind the Filter Change steps (maintenance page) and the Filter Cleaning section (reference page). The step text itself lives in `shared/maintenance.ts`. Compiled 2026-11-14. Each claim is tagged **sourced** (link), **computed**, **inferred**, or **measured (date)**.

## Setup

- Filter: POOLPURE C-5345 (Unicel C-5345 equivalent, pleated polyester). Two are rotated. **measured (2026-11-14, owner's account)**
- Rinse tool: Filter Flosser. **measured (2026-11-14, owner's account)**
- Change cadence in the app: every 30 days (`MAINTENANCE_CADENCE_DAYS.filter_change`).

## Don't soak in bleach

- Bleach is a poor cleaner and harsh on filter media: [Sundance](https://www.sundancespas.com/blog-articles/how-to-clean-a-hot-tub-filter-using-sundance-r-spa-filters-the-right-way) ("too harsh — eats away at filter media"), the Pleatco FilterWash FAQ (search snippet; the [page](https://www.pleatco.com/filterwash) now redirects), and the [Bath and Spa Parts guide](https://www.bathandspaparts.com/blogs/news/spa-pool-filter-cartridge-cleaning-guide). **sourced**
- [Hot Spring's sheet](https://www.hotspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/hot-spring-spas-how-to-clean-hot-tub-spa-filter.pdf) says rinse, spray with filter cleaner or degreaser, and soak overnight, with no mention of bleach. **sourced**
- No manufacturer manual was found that prescribes a bleach soak. **sourced** (absence in the sources checked)

## Degreaser soak, then rinse

- Soak times: Hot Spring overnight, Sundance 8–12 h, Leisure Time 3 h minimum with overnight best. **sourced** (links above and below)
- Never combine filter cleaner or bleach with an acid cleaner (Unicel warning, search snippet; retailer guide). **sourced** (weaker)
- Unicel's older instructions allow dishwasher detergent; Sundance says to avoid it. **sourced**

## Cleaners

- **[Leisure Time Filter Clean](https://shop.leisuretimeinc.com/products/filter-clean)** (also on Amazon): 16 oz per 4 gal, 3 h minimum, overnight best. Alkaline, pH 12–13 (search snippet). **sourced**

## The steps, and where each comes from

1. Pump off, pull the dirty filter.
2. Put in the clean, dry spare; pump on. Rotating two filters is what makes the overnight soak possible. **inferred**
3. Rinse the dirty one with the Filter Flosser, top to bottom, turning it, until the water runs clear. No pressure washer or stiff brush: both damage pleats. **inferred**
4. Soak fully submerged overnight (3 h minimum) in filter cleaner: TSP 1 cup per 5 gal, or Leisure Time Filter Clean 16 oz per 4 gal. **sourced** (Hot Spring, Sundance, Leisure Time, Unicel)
5. Rinse until there are no suds and no slick feel. **inferred**
6. Dry completely; store out of the sun. **inferred**
7. No routine bleach soak; never mix bleach or filter cleaner with an acid cleaner. **sourced** (see above)
8. Replace when pleats flatten or fray, end caps crack, or bands break (about 12–24 months). **inferred** (common retailer guidance; no single source pinned here)
